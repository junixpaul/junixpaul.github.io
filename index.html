About

Hi, I'm Junpaul. I'm a product owner who started out as a developer, so I feel most at home between the people who use a product and the people who build it.

After earning my IT degree at Bukidnon State University, I joined Wela School Systems as a web developer in 2018. I grew with the team for almost seven years, moving from web developer to senior developer to product owner. Along the way I learned to take messy requests from schools and business teams and turn them into clear user stories a developer can pick up and ship.

Having written the code myself helps a lot. I can talk honestly with engineers about trade-offs and explain them to stakeholders in plain language. I keep the customer in mind throughout, so the team spends its time on what actually helps them. I recently earned a certification in product owner communication and stakeholder management to sharpen that side of my work.

Today I'm the product owner at Livro Systems Inc. in Cagayan de Oro City, where I lead the team building a school management system on Frappe/ERPNext.

I still love building things on my own time. Paws is a budgeting app with a cat that hands out money tips, GanttFlow brings Gantt chart planning into ERPNext, and Pickit is a browser game made with Phaser.
